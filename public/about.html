Our Story

Visualizing the Invisible

From the shores of Olango Island to classrooms across Cebu and Region 7, Sulbad STEM transforms abstract concepts into clear, localized educational experiences.

Our Origin Story

Sulbad STEM was born on Olango Island, where we witnessed firsthand how students struggled with abstract concepts in Calculus and Physics—not from lack of intelligence, but from a shortage of visual learning resources. Teachers had the knowledge, but lacked the specialized tools to make complex theories tangible.

The word "Sulbad" means "Solution" in Cebuano. We exist to solve the understanding gap by turning complex STEM theories into clear, culturally relevant illustrations that resonate with Filipino learners.

Our Mission

To bridge the digital divide in Philippine education through specialized content development, making world-class STEM visualizations accessible to every school in Cebu, Visayas, and beyond.
